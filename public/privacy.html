Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 4, 2026  ·  Entity: Suvarna Enterprise ERP Solutions Pvt. Ltd. (TallyPrime ERP Core)

This Privacy Policy describes how Suvarna Enterprise ERP Solutions Pvt. Ltd. ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, processes, and protects your personal data and commercial records when you interact with TallyPrime ERP Core Replica (the "Service") at https://tallyprime-enterprise.local or local instances.

1. Data Minimization & Accounting Records

We adhere to the global principle of Data Minimization under GDPR, India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA 2023), and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). We only collect and process information strictly necessary for double-entry financial accounting and statutory compliance:

  • Operator & Auditor Identity: Username, cryptographic role assignments (Admin, Data Entry, Auditor, Owner), and hashed password digests (stored with PBKDF2 with unique salts; plaintext passwords are never stored or transmitted).
  • Commercial & Statutory Data: Business entity names, GSTINs, PANs, ledger accounts, stock balances, and financial vouchers created pursuant to Indian statutory requirements (Companies Act 2013 and GST Rules).
  • Audit Trail Logging: Immutable event timestamps, user IDs, and SHA-256 cryptographic hashes logged in accordance with Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) Audit Trail Rule 3(1).

2. Lawful Basis for Processing

We process your information under the following recognized legal grounds:

  • Statutory Compliance: Compliance with Indian financial reporting laws, MCA audit mandate, and GSTN filing regulations.
  • Contractual Necessity: Delivering double-entry bookkeeping, multi-user concurrency, and database integrity services.
  • Explicit Affirmative Consent: Freely given consent for application usage, session preferences, and communications.

3. Local Storage, Cookies & Cryptographic Security

We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal or financial data. Authentication tokens and ledger caches utilize secure local storage and memory caches protected with AES-256-GCM encryption in TallyVault mode. For complete details, see our Cookie Policy.

4. Your Legal Rights (DPDPA, GDPR & CCPA)

Subject to applicable law and MCA statutory record retention obligations (minimum 8-year accounting retention rule under Companies Act 2013), you have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal data and export your ledger registers in XML/JSON format.
  • Request correction of inaccurate personal contact details.
  • Revoke consent for non-statutory processing and manage cookie settings.
  • Lodge a complaint with the Data Protection Board of India or your local supervisory authority.
