Cookie Policy

Effective Date: October 4, 2026  ·  Entity: Suvarna Enterprise ERP Solutions Pvt. Ltd.

This Cookie Policy explains how Suvarna Enterprise ERP Solutions Pvt. Ltd. uses cookies and client storage mechanisms in TallyPrime ERP Core Replica. It complies with the ePrivacy Directive, GDPR, and India DPDPA regulations.

1. What Are Cookies and Client Storage?

Cookies and LocalStorage objects are secure data records stored directly within your web browser to preserve system security, maintain user sessions, and remember operational ERP views between reloads.

2. Complete Inventory of Storage Items

Key / Cookie Classification Duration Purpose
site_cookie_consent_v1 Strictly Necessary 1 Year Records user opt-in/opt-out preferences for privacy banner.
tally_auth_session Strictly Necessary Session (8 hrs) Preserves authenticated cryptographic token (Bearer sess_...) to prevent unauthorized session hijacking.
tally_store_v1 Strictly Necessary Persistent Local double-entry database cache maintaining chart of accounts and vouchers in browser offline storage.
tally_user_preferences Functional 6 Months Stores UI date context (F2), active voucher mode, and drilldown depth.

3. Zero Unsolicited Third-Party Tracking

TallyPrime ERP does not deploy third-party advertising pixels, cross-site trackers, or data brokers (e.g., Meta Pixel, TikTok Pixel, Google Ads). All operational telemetry is self-contained within your local or enterprise network perimeter.
